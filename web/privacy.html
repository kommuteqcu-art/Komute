Komute Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Komute is a free Quezon City commute helper at komute.onrender.com and its Android app.

What we collect

  • Account details: if you sign in with Google or email, we receive your email address and name so we can create your account.
  • Saved places and trips: places you save are stored with your account so you can see them on other devices.
  • Location: only if you tap "My location". It is used to set your starting point and is not stored on our servers.
  • Payment details: if you pay for Komute Plus, we keep the GCash reference number and amount you submit so we can confirm your payment.

How we use it

Only to run Komute: sign you in, save your places, and confirm payments. We do not sell your data or show ads.

Who handles it

Sign-in and storage are provided by Supabase. Google sign-in is provided by Google. The site is hosted on Render.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and data, email kommuteqcu@gmail.com.
