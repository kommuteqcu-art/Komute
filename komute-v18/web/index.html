Before you ride

  • Check the vehicle’s route sign matches your plan.
  • Ask the driver or conductor for the current fare.
  • Carry small bills or coins.
  • Tell someone your route if the trip is new to you.
This guide is AI-made. Routes, fares, and schedules may have changed. Always verify locally.
