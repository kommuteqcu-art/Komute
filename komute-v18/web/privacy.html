Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Komute (komute.onrender.com) is a free commute guide for Quezon City, made as a Quezon City University student project. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google or with an email, we receive your email address and name. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Trip searches: the starting point and destination you type, so we can plan your route.
  • Location: only if you allow it in your browser or phone, to show where you are on the map. It is not stored.
  • Usage: how many free route plans an account has used, and a random device number (not your phone's ID) that we use to limit free plans to one account per device.
  • Payments: if you pay for unlimited plans, the payment reference number you send us. We do not see or store your GCash or bank login.

How we use it

To sign you in, plan routes, count free plans, confirm payments and keep the service from being abused. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who processes it

Sign-in and account data are handled by Supabase. Maps and routing use OpenStreetMap-based services. Route planning text may be processed by an AI service to build your route. These services receive only what is needed for their job.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. You can also delete your account inside the app (Account → Delete my account). This closes the account and signs you out everywhere, but we keep your free-plan count, payment records and device link so that signing in again with the same Google account restores them, and so free plans cannot be reset by deleting an account. To ask us to erase this information completely, email kommuteqcu@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with.

Children

Komute is a general commute guide and is not directed at young children.
